# Scribuild — "Huff & Puff" (:30)

2D hand-drawn cartoon spot. Pencil linework with loose watercolour fills on mostly blank off-white paper. Muted palette, "boiling" lines (re-drawn at 8 fps), minimalist European ad-cartoon style. Deadpan timing, near-static camera, hard cuts. No brands or logos other than Scribuild.

**Files**
- `index.html`: interactive animatic (play, scrub, beat jump, captions, scratch voice via browser TTS). Open it in any browser.
- `scribuild-huff-and-puff-30s.mp4`: 1920×1080, 30 fps render of the animatic with captions burned in. No audio track yet; VO and SFX go in at the mix.

## Cast
- **Third Little Pig**: contractor. Tool belt with hammer, pencil behind the ear, permanently heavy-lidded.
- **Big Bad Wolf**: the homeowner. Mustard cardigan, coffee mug, very polite menace.
- **Narrator**: dry, light European accent.

## VO notes
- Wolf's long line at 0:09.3 is tight: about 1.7 s for three demands. Deliver it fast and flat, like reading a list. The joke is that he's too polite to be menacing.
- Leave a full beat of silence between "very thorough." and "So… what do I huff now?"
- Narrator reads the tag line unhurried, with no upward sell.
